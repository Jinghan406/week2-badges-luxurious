Seal & Card

Notices that arrive like coins, and answers that arrive by card.

Week II · Set V · Tone Luxurious

I

The Badge

Nothing shouts. A pearl settles on what is new; a gold coin turns over when the count changes.

Without content

The pearl

Tap a medallion to place or lift it.

With content

Coin, ribbon, star

A count is minted on a coin; when it changes, the coin turns over.

II

The Tooltip

A line of gold rises with a quiet inscription; an invitation is presented, and sealed when you accept.

Plain

The inscription

Hover a medallion. A gold thread rises and the words come into focus.

Rich · with action

The invitation

Hover or tap. Accept, and the card is sealed in wax — the coin turns over.

Coin — minted

Flips in from edge-on, 560 ms, ease-out-quint; a gleam crosses the face.

Coin — turned over

Spins to its edge in 220 ms, shows the new number on the way back, 320 ms.

Pearl — placed / lifted

Settles from 10 px above, 520 ms; lifts and fades, 320 ms.

Inscription

After 300 ms, a 30 px gold thread grows; letters sharpen from a 6 px blur, 22 ms apart.

Invitation — presented

Rises 24 px from behind the medallion, 600 ms; lines appear 60 ms apart.

Invitation — sealed

Wax lands from 180% to 100% with a press, 420 ms; the next card follows.
